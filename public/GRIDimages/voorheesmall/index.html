Voorhees Mall

Role: Portrait Illustrator

This Extended Reality experience was designed to be a showcase for the possibilities of Augmented Reality technology. Our team was commissioned by The Society for College and University Planning conference at Rutgers University.

Our goal was a multipronged approach. I was tasked with creating characters for our tour. They represent buildings and the departments within. Players embark on a self-guided tour and meet with the characters to learn more aboout the campus history.

In the end, we showcased the rich history of the buildings at Voorhees Mall. We also demonstrated the diverse community of Rutgers University.
